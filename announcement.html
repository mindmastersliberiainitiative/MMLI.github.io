📢 Practice World Mathematics Team Championship (PWMTC) 2026 – Call for Participants

The Mind Masters Liberia Initiative (MMLI) is inviting applications from students interested in representing our team at the Practice World Mathematics Team Championship (PWMTC) 2026. Our team will be writing this test in our own region and country.

Eligibility:

  • Junior Level: Students born on or after 2014
  • Intermediate Level: Students born on or after 2011
  • Advanced Level: Students born on or after 2008

📅 Competition Period: August 3 – September 30, 2026 (our team's exact competition date will be announced by the coach)

⚠️ Only 10 students will be selected to represent our team, based on eligibility, commitment, and readiness for the competition.

📞 To register: Contact us at 0889581634 as soon as possible. Applications close once all 10 spots are filled.

📢 Olympiad Training Program Registration Now Open!

The Mind Masters Liberia Initiative is excited to announce the opening of registration for our Olympiad Training Program. This program is designed to help students build strong problem-solving skills in Mathematics and other core academic areas through structured training, practice, and mentorship. Training is conducted under The Liberia National Olympiad Foundation.

🎯 Who can join:

  • Junior Students
  • Senior Students
  • Beginners with interest in Mathematics and Science

📍 Training will cover:

  • Olympiad Mathematics Techniques (Number Theory, Advanced Algebra, Euclidean Geometry, Combinatorics)
  • Problem Solving Skills
  • Competitive Exam Preparation

📢 Back-to-School Academic Quiz Challenge 2026

Theme: "Unleashing the Genius Within"

MMLI is proud to announce the Back-to-School Academic Quiz Challenge 2026, an inter-school competition open to 16 Junior High and 16 Senior High schools across Liberia. Only 32 schools will be accepted, so registration closes once all slots are filled.

Subjects covered:

Mathematics, English Language, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Integrated Science, Information Technology, General Knowledge, Current Affairs, and Logical Reasoning.

Registration fees:

  • Junior High: LRD 1,500
  • Senior High: LRD 2,000

Awards:

Champion Trophy, Gold Medals, Certificates of Achievement, and Special Recognition for the Champion School.
