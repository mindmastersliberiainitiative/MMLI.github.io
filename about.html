About Us

Mind Masters Liberia Initiative (MMLI) is a nonprofit educational organization dedicated to discovering, developing, and empowering young minds through mathematics, science, innovation, leadership, and academic excellence.

Founded on June 10, 2025, MMLI was established with a vision to create a platform where talented students across Liberia can access mentorship, advanced academic training, intellectual competitions, and opportunities that enable them to reach their full potential.

We believe that every student has unique abilities and untapped potential. Through our programs, we work to identify promising talents, strengthen critical thinking skills, encourage creativity, and inspire a culture of excellence among young people.

MMLI focuses on building the next generation of mathematicians, scientists, innovators, researchers, and leaders by providing educational experiences that go beyond traditional classroom learning. Through mathematics and science training, Olympiad preparation, academic competitions, mentorship programs, STEM initiatives, and leadership development, we prepare students to compete and succeed at national and international levels.

Our Vision

To become Liberia's leading center for academic excellence and talent development, recognized across Africa for nurturing exceptional thinkers, innovators, and future leaders.

Our Mission

To discover, train, mentor, and empower young minds through quality education, academic competitions, STEM programs, and leadership opportunities that develop critical thinkers capable of creating meaningful solutions for society.

Founder & Executive Director

B. Hallison F. Thomas

The Mind Masters Liberia Initiative (MMLI) was founded on June 10, 2025, by B. Hallison F. Thomas, a Liberian mathematician, educator, and youth academic leader with a passion for transforming education through excellence and innovation.

The idea behind MMLI was inspired by a simple but powerful belief: talent exists everywhere, but opportunity determines how far talent can go. While engaging with students and young scholars, he recognized that many gifted students with strong abilities in mathematics, science, and critical thinking lacked access to advanced training, mentorship, and platforms where their talents could be recognized and developed.

To address this challenge, MMLI was created as a national movement focused on identifying exceptional students, providing academic support, and building a community where intellectual achievement is valued and celebrated. Since its establishment, MMLI has expanded its work beyond mathematics competitions to include academic enrichment programs, Olympiad training, science initiatives, mentorship, leadership development, and educational outreach.

Mind Masters Liberia Initiative is more than an organization—it is a movement committed to unleashing the genius within every learner.
